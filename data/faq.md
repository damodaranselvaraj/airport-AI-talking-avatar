# FAQ — keep answers short enough to speak

## Is DIA open 24 hours?

Yes. The building is open all night. T2 security is 24 hours. T1 and T3 security close at midnight and reopen at 04:00. Many shops and desks close earlier — ask if you need a specific desk.

## Where is gate C22?

Gate C22 is in Terminal 2, Concourse C, Level 2. From T2 security it is about a 12-minute walk. The nearest café is Date Palm Café, two minutes before the gate.

## Where is the prayer room?

The main prayer room is Terminal 2, Level 1, airside, in the corridor toward gates B8 to B10. It is open 24 hours. Terminal 1 has a smaller airside room left of gate A4. Terminal 3 has a landside room next to the information desk.

## Where is lost and found?

Desk 4, Terminal 2 Arrivals, Level 0, open 06:00 to 22:00. The phone is +971-0-000-3344. Items from T1 and T3 are sent to this same desk.

## How do I get to the city?

The DIA Metro Red Line starts under Terminal 1. First train 04:45, last train to the city 00:30, about 28 minutes to City Centre. Official taxis are on Arrivals Level 0 under the yellow canopy. Hotel buses leave from Zone E between T2 and T3.

## How early should I arrive?

For Terminal 2 international, be at check-in 3 hours before departure; the row closes 60 minutes before. For T1, rows close 45 minutes before. T2 security after 17:00 can take 25 minutes.

## Can I take water through security?

You may take liquids of 100 ml or less, all in one 1-litre clear bag. Fill a bottle after security. Water fountains are at the gates, including next to Date Palm Café near C22.

## Is there a family security lane?

Yes, Terminal 2 only, left of the standard lanes, 05:00 to 22:00, for children under 12 or anyone who needs more time. There is no paid fast-track.

## I am connecting. Do I go through security again?

If you stay airside and follow purple Connections signs on the Skywalk, you do not repeat security. If you exit to arrivals, you will have to screen again.

## Does this guide know my flight time or gate today?

No. This guide only knows the building. Live times and last-minute gate changes are on the departure screens and at the airline desk.
