# Dam International Airport — terminals

Dam International Airport (IATA: **DIA**) has three passenger terminals connected airside by the Skywalk moving walkway. Allow **8 minutes** to walk T1↔T2 and **10 minutes** to walk T2↔T3. The Skywalk is open 24 hours.

## Terminal 1 — Domestic and regional

Terminal 1 (T1) handles domestic flights and short regional hops.

- **Airlines:** DamAir (DIA), Pearl Wings (PW)
- **Gates:** A1–A20, all on Level 2 after security
- **Check-in:** Level 1, rows 1–12. Opens 3 hours before departure; closes 45 minutes before departure
- **Arrivals:** Level 0, baggage belts 1–6
- **Best for:** travelers staying inside the country or flying to nearby cities

DamAir is the home carrier. Its ticket desk is T1 Level 1, opposite row 4.

## Terminal 2 — International long-haul

Terminal 2 (T2) is the main international terminal.

- **Airlines:** Horizon Air (HZ), Cedar Pacific (CP), Sahara Jet (SJ)
- **Gates:** B1–B30 on Concourse B; **C1–C24 on Concourse C**
- **Check-in:** Level 1, rows 20–48. Opens 4 hours before departure; closes 60 minutes before departure
- **Arrivals:** Level 0, baggage belts 7–18, plus the international meet-and-greet hall
- **Best for:** long-haul and most connecting international flights

Immigration and customs for arriving international passengers are only in T2 and T3, not in T1.

## Terminal 3 — Low-cost and extra international

Terminal 3 (T3) is the low-cost and overflow international terminal.

- **Airlines:** Sandpiper (SP), DIA Express (NX)
- **Gates:** D1–D18, Level 2 after security
- **Check-in:** Level 1, rows 50–62. Opens 3 hours before departure; closes 50 minutes before departure
- **Arrivals:** Level 0, baggage belts 19–24
- **Best for:** low-cost carriers and some seasonal international flights

## Changing terminals

- **Airside (already through security, connecting to another flight):** use the Skywalk. Follow purple “Connections” signs. Do not exit to arrivals or you will have to clear security again.
- **Landside (before security, or you have landed and are leaving):** use the free landside shuttle every 8 minutes, or walk the arrivals corridor (about 15 minutes T1 to T3).

This document does not list live departures. Gate letters can move. If a boarding pass disagrees with this guide, trust the boarding pass and the screens.
