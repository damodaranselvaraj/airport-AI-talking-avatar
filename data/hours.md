# Hours and phone numbers

The airport building is **open 24 hours**. Not every desk is.

| Service | Where | Hours | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information desk (main) | T2 Level 1 | 05:00–00:00 | +971-0-000-1100 |
| Information desk T1 | T1 Level 1 | 05:00–00:00 | use main number |
| Information desk T3 | T3 Level 1 | 05:00–23:00 | use main number |
| Lost and found | T2 Arrivals desk 4 | 06:00–22:00 | +971-0-000-3344 |
| Medical clinic | T2 Level 0 landside | 24 hours | radio via yellow help point |
| Family security lane | T2 only | 05:00–22:00 | — |
| Date Palm Café (near C22) | T2 Concourse C | 05:00–22:00 | — |
| Zone E hotel-shuttle desk | between T2 and T3 | 06:00–23:00 | — |
| T2 security checkpoint | T2 Level 1 | 24 hours | — |
| T1 and T3 security | Level 1 | 04:00–00:00 | — |
| First metro to City Centre | T1 station | 04:45 | — |
| Last metro to City Centre | T1 station | 00:30 | — |
| Last metro from City Centre to DIA | City Centre | 00:00 | — |

Check-in row opening times are in `terminals.md` (3 or 4 hours before departure, depending on the terminal).

If a desk is closed, use the yellow help points (one in every arrivals hall and every concourse). They connect to night operations, not to airlines.
