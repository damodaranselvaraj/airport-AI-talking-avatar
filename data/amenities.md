# Amenities at DIA

## Prayer rooms

- **Terminal 2, Level 1, airside** — past the shops, corridor toward gates B8–B10. Open 24 hours. Wudu area attached. This is the main prayer room.
- **Terminal 1, Level 2, airside** — after security, left of gate A4. Open 04:30–00:00.
- **Terminal 3, Level 1, landside** — arrivals, next to the information desk. Open 05:00–23:00.

There is no prayer room landside in T2. Use the T2 airside room after security, or the T3 landside room if you have not checked in yet.

## Lost and found

**Desk 4, Terminal 2 Arrivals, Level 0.**

- Hours: **06:00–22:00** every day
- Phone: **+971-0-000-3344** (desk only; no WhatsApp)
- Items found in T1 or T3 are brought to this same T2 desk by 14:00 the next day
- Hold time: 30 days, then donated
- For items left **on an aircraft**, go to that airline’s ticket desk, not lost and found

## Medical

The clinic is **Terminal 2, Level 0, landside**, beside the arrivals exit, marked with a red cross.

- Open **24 hours**
- For a medical emergency anywhere in the airport, use a yellow help point or ask any staff member. They radio the clinic
- The clinic does not issue fit-to-fly letters for missed flights. That is the airline’s decision

## Nursing / family room

Terminal 2, Level 1, airside, opposite the family security lane exit. Open 05:00–22:00. Sink, chairs, and a lockable door. No reservation.

## Wifi and charging

- Network name: **DIA-Free**
- No password. Accept the portal once per device
- Fair-use cap: 2 hours, then reconnect
- Charging: every gate hold room has sockets on alternate rows; extra banks at Date Palm Café (C22 area) and the T1 food court

## Information desks

- T1 Level 1 check-in hall, **05:00–00:00**
- T2 Level 1 check-in hall (main desk), **05:00–00:00**, phone **+971-0-000-1100**
- T3 Level 1 check-in hall, **05:00–23:00**

Desks do not rebook flights and do not give immigration rulings.
