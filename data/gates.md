# Gates at DIA

Gates are grouped by terminal. The letter is the concourse. The number is the door.

| Concourse | Terminal | Gate range | Level after security |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | T1 | A1–A20 | 2 |
| B | T2 | B1–B30 | 2 |
| C | T2 | C1–C24 | 2 |
| D | T3 | D1–D18 | 2 |

Last call at DIA is **15 minutes before the scheduled departure** unless the airline desk says otherwise. After last call the door will not reopen for late travelers.

## Gate C22 (demo gate)

**Gate C22 is in Terminal 2, Concourse C, Level 2.**

- From T2 security (central checkpoint): follow signs for **Gates C**, then walk past C12. Allow **12 minutes**.
- From T1 via Skywalk: enter T2 at the B/C split, turn right for C. Allow **20 minutes** from T1 security.
- From T3 via Skywalk: walk through T2 Concourse B, then C. Allow **22 minutes** from T3 security.
- **Nearest food:** Date Palm Café, 2 minutes before C22, open 05:00–22:00
- **Nearest restrooms:** opposite C20
- **Nearest water fountain:** next to Date Palm Café
- **Seating:** 80 seats at the hold room; power sockets on every other row
- **Boarding:** usually begins 40 minutes before departure; last call 15 minutes before departure

If a screen shows C22 for a different airline than Horizon Air, Cedar Pacific, or Sahara Jet, the flight was moved. Recheck the boarding pass.

## Other useful gates

- **A1–A8:** closest T1 gates; 4 minutes from T1 security. Pearl Wings uses A1–A6 often.
- **A12–A20:** far T1 pier; allow 10 minutes from T1 security.
- **B1–B10:** closest T2 gates; 5 minutes from T2 security. Horizon Air short-haul often uses B4–B9.
- **B24–B30:** far B pier; allow 14 minutes. Bus-boarding doors B28–B30 go to remote stands.
- **C1–C8:** first C cluster; 8 minutes from T2 security.
- **C18–C24:** far C cluster (includes C22); 12 minutes from T2 security.
- **D1–D8:** closest T3 gates; 5 minutes from T3 security. Sandpiper uses D2–D6 often.
- **D14–D18:** far T3; allow 11 minutes. Some D16–D18 departures use a bus to the aircraft.

## Bus gates (remote stands)

Gates **B28, B29, B30** and **D16, D17, D18** may board by bus. The hold room is still at that gate number. Follow staff to the bus dock one level below. Add **10 extra minutes**.

This document does not know which flight is at which gate right now. Ask the airline desk or read the departure screens for live assignments.
