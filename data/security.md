# Security at DIA

Every departing passenger goes through security. There is one central checkpoint per terminal, all on Level 1, before the escalators to the gates.

## What to take out

- **Laptops and large tablets** go in a separate tray. Keep them out of bags.
- **Liquids, gels, and aerosols** in carry-on: each container **100 ml or smaller**, all inside one clear resealable bag of **1 litre**. One bag per traveler.
- **Belts, coins, phones, and keys** go in the tray. Jackets off.
- **Duty-free liquids** bought airside at DIA may stay sealed in the shop’s STEB bag. Liquids bought at a previous airport may be confiscated if the bag is open — ask the officer.

## Lanes

- **Standard lane:** all terminals, 04:00–00:00 (T2 is 24 hours).
- **Family lane:** Terminal 2 only, left of the standard lanes. Travelers with children under 12 or a passenger who needs more time. Open 05:00–22:00.
- **Accessible lane:** all terminals, marked with the wheelchair sign. Ask any officer.
- There is **no paid fast-track** at DIA.

Typical wait is 10–20 minutes. After 17:00 in T2, plan for 25 minutes.

## What security will not do

Officers do not give visa, immigration, or customs advice. They will not tell you whether you may enter a country. That is immigration, not this guide.

If you are connecting and already cleared security at DIA, you do not repeat screening unless you leave the airside area.
