# Getting to and from DIA

## DIA Metro (Red Line)

The metro station is **under Terminal 1**. From T2 or T3, follow orange “Metro” signs through the landside arrivals corridor or take the free landside shuttle to T1.

- First train toward City Centre: **04:45**
- Last train toward City Centre: **00:30**
- Last train toward the airport from City Centre: **00:00**
- Journey to City Centre: about **28 minutes**
- Tickets: contactless bank card or the DIA Metro app. There is no cash window after 22:00

The metro does not run to individual hotels. Use taxi or hotel shuttle after City Centre.

## Taxi rank

Official DIA taxis wait on **Arrivals Level 0** outside each terminal, under the yellow “Taxi” canopy.

- T1 rank: 8 cars
- T2 rank: 20 cars (busiest)
- T3 rank: 8 cars
- Night surcharge after 22:00 is printed on the meter card in the window
- Refuse unmarked cars in the car park. Only taxis at the official rank are licensed here

A typical ride to City Centre is 35–50 minutes depending on traffic. This guide does not quote fares; they change.

## Car parks

| Park | Who it is for | Walk to terminal | Shuttle |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Short stay, T1 | 3 minutes | No |
| P2 | Short stay, T2 | 4 minutes | No |
| P3 | Short stay, T3 | 3 minutes | No |
| P4 Long stay | All terminals | — | Every 6 minutes, 24 hours, to all arrivals doors |

P4 is west of T3. Follow “Long stay P4” from the highway spur. Keep your entry ticket; payment is at the barrier or the P4 machines.

## Hotel shuttle (Zone E)

Hotel buses pick up at **Zone E**, landside, between T2 and T3 arrivals.

- Follow green “Hotel shuttle / Zone E” signs from any arrivals hall (5–8 minutes on foot)
- Each hotel has a marked bay. Staff at the Zone E desk (06:00–23:00) can point to the bay
- If your hotel is not on the board, use the taxi rank

This document does not book rides or know live traffic.
